## Overview / О проекте

This repository hosts the LaTeX source files and preprints for the research paper **"Topological Architecture of Mental Causation: Sheaf Theory, Étale Spaces, and Gauge Connections in the Theory of Substrateless Onto-Information"** (*«Топологическая архитектура ментальной каузальности: аппарат пучков, этальных пространств и калибровочных связностей в теории бессубстратной онто-информации»*).

The study addresses Jaegwon Kim’s **causal exclusion problem** by replacing classical force-based (energetic) models of mind–matter interaction with a topological framework grounded in **ontic structural realism**, **sheaf theory**, and **fiber bundles**.

## Key Contributions / Основные положения

* **Étale Space Architecture ($E_{\text{LF}} \to X_{\text{AF}}$):** Physical spacetime is modeled as the base space of an *Action Framework* ($X_{\text{AF}}$ / $X_{\text{КД}}$), fibered beneath the étale space of a *Logic Framework* ($E_{\text{LF}}$ / $E_{\text{ЛК}}$) containing pure relational invariants.
* **Gauge Degeneracy of Least Action ($\delta \mathcal{S} = 0$):** At the neurophysiological micro-level, the variational functional of the Principle of Least Action exhibits fundamental gauge degeneracy, generating an orbit of thermodynamically equivalent trajectories.
* **Qualia as a Noetic Macrocode ($\Psi_{\text{noet}}$):** Phenomenal experience operates as an ultra-compressed quotienting functor optimized to bypass Landauer’s thermodynamic limit ($W_{\min} = k_{\text{B}} T \ln 2$), acting as an energy-free vertical gauge selector ($g \in G_{\text{LF}}$).
* **Connection Reconfiguration & Cohomology:** Volitional agency modifies the gauge connection of Synchronous Informational Resonance ($\mathcal{D}_{\text{SIR}}$) and trivializes Čech cohomology obstructions ($\check{H}^1$), guiding Synchronous Onto-Initiations (SOI) along a chosen horizontal lift.
* **Strict Causal Closure ($\nabla_\mu T^{\mu\nu} = 0$):** The physical stress-energy tensor remains strictly divergence-free, dissolving the dilemma between epiphenomenalism and quantum stochasticity while overcoming the computational and thermal limits of **IIT** and **Orch-OR**.
